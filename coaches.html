ASSISTANT COACHES

Luke Shaw

Assistant Coach

"From First Athlete to First Coach"

Luke Shaw's journey with Panthera is one of the most inspiring stories in the club. He was the very first athlete to register at Panthera Athletic Club — and at just 16 years old, he became the club's first assistant coach. His dedication, growth mindset, and natural leadership made him the perfect fit to help shape the next generation of Panthera athletes.

Assignments: Panthera Soccer Academy, Panthera Basketball Academy

NCCP Ontario Basketball (in training) Ontario Soccer (in training)
"Luke Shaw isn't just coaching our athletes — he's showing them what's possible when you believe in yourself."

VOLUNTEER COACHES

Phoenix Burns

Volunteer Coach / Team Photographer

"Bringing Energy and Fun to Panthera Volleyball Academy"
